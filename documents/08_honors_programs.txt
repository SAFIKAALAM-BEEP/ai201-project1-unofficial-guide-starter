MACAULAY HONORS COLLEGE AT HUNTER
Source: Scholarships page (hunter.cuny.edu)
Description: The Macaulay Honors College provides outstanding educational opportunities for academically gifted students from the five boroughs of New York City and beyond. It is designed for students who have demonstrated scholastic strength and broad intellectual curiosity through their high school academic records, standardized test scores, extracurricular involvements, and other academic criteria. Macaulay Honors College is on eight CUNY campuses: Baruch College, Brooklyn College, City College, College of Staten Island, Hunter College, John Jay College of Criminal Justice, Lehman College, and Queens College.

Financial Aid Package:
- Full tuition each year (students must meet CUNY NYS residency requirements)
- Access to study grants from sophomore through senior year for academically enriched experiences such as study abroad, living expenses during unpaid internships, or research projects
- A free laptop computer

Application Requirements:
- High school grades (strong GPA required)
- SAT I / ACT scores
- An essay
- Recommendations
- In some cases, an interview
- Apply via the CUNY Application for Macaulay Honors College at Hunter College

To continue receiving the Macaulay financial package, students must remain in good academic standing as defined by the Macaulay Honors College.

Note: The Macaulay scholarship is drawn from a combination of sources: CUNY, TAP, City/State scholarships, and other tuition-only scholarships the student may have received.

THOMAS HUNTER HONORS PROGRAM
The Thomas Hunter Honors Program is Hunter College's own honors program for undergraduate students already enrolled at Hunter. It offers smaller, discussion-based honors sections of regular courses, a community of high-achieving peers, priority registration, and recognition at graduation. Students in Thomas Hunter Honors gain priority enrollment status, which is a significant advantage for course registration.

Eligibility: Typically requires a GPA of 3.5 or higher and faculty recommendation.

COOPERATIVE EDUCATION PROGRAM (CO-OP)
Hunter's Cooperative Education Program allows students to alternate between academic semesters and paid work experience in their field. It is particularly popular among students in the sciences, business, and health professions.

CSTEP (Collegiate Science and Technology Entry Program)
CSTEP is a New York State-funded program that supports historically underrepresented and economically disadvantaged students pursuing careers in science, technology, engineering, mathematics, and the health professions. Benefits include academic advisement, tutoring, research opportunities, and professional development workshops.

SEEK (Search for Education, Elevation and Knowledge)
SEEK is CUNY's opportunity program for students from low-income backgrounds who show academic potential but may not meet standard admissions requirements. SEEK students receive financial aid supplements, counseling, tutoring, and additional support services. Hunter's SEEK program is one of the largest in the CUNY system.

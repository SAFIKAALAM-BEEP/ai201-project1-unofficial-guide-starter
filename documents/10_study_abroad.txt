--- GETTING STARTED ---

Before applying for a program abroad, review the Eligibility Requirements and ensure that you have either: (1) attended a Study Abroad 101 session, (2) watched the recording of the most recent Study Abroad 101 session, or (3) met with program staff for a one-on-one advising meeting.

Documents to prepare for your application:
- Personal statement: 250–500 words describing how study abroad contributes to your academic or professional goals and how you plan to engage with the local community abroad. For semester- or year-long exchange programs, prepare a 1–2 page personal statement responding to the same prompt.
- Unofficial transcript (can be pulled from CUNYfirst; your name must appear on it)
- Scan of the photo page of your valid passport. If you are applying for or renewing your passport, you may submit the application without it, but must email a scan as soon as it arrives. All students must have a valid passport by their program's orientation session.

After acceptance, all students must submit:
- Health Information Questionnaire and Physician Statement (start this early — it may take time to get a physician appointment)
- CUNY Waiver and Release Agreement
- GDPR Acknowledgement and Consent Form (for programs in the EU)
- Proof of SPARC course enrollment (administered by CUNY through the Title IX office)

--- PROGRAM DEPOSIT ---

Once accepted to any Hunter study abroad program, you must submit a $350 Money Order or Certified Check made out to HUNTER COLLEGE. In the memo line, write your first and last name and the name of the program. This deposit is applied to the overall program fee.

Important deposit notes:
- If you withdraw before the application deadline, you will be refunded your deposit minus $50 (kept as the application fee).
- If you withdraw after the application deadline, the deposit is not refundable.
- If you withdraw after the start date, you will be responsible for the entire program fee.

--- SEMESTER EXCHANGE PROGRAMS ---

Exchange programs allow you to study at an overseas university while continuing to pay tuition at the Hunter College rate. In exchange, a student from the overseas university studies at Hunter. Exchanges provide full immersion at a foreign university — students take classes with students from the host country.

Exchange programs are most appropriate for independent and highly motivated students looking for a fully immersive study abroad experience. Students must adapt to what is often a different, more autonomous educational structure.

Specific Requirements for Exchange Programs:
- Minimum 3.0 GPA at the time of application
- At least 60 completed credits at the end of the semester in which you are applying (most commonly, students apply during their third year at Hunter)
- Full-time course load while abroad (equivalent of 12 Hunter credits)
- All exchange programs have limited placements and can be quite competitive

Application Deadlines for Exchange Programs:
- For Spring Semesters: applications must be received by September 15 the semester prior to intended departure
- For Fall Semesters and Academic Year: applications must be received by February 15 the semester prior to intended departure

Hunter recommends having a second, non-exchange program as a backup if applying to an exchange, in case you are not selected as a nominee.

--- CUNY PROGRAMS ---

Hunter students may apply to study abroad programs administered by other CUNY schools, provided the program is ePermit eligible. Search the CUNY program search engine to explore other CUNY schools' offerings.

All Hunter College students MUST submit the "CUNY Program" application in the Hunter study abroad online portal before applying to the respective CUNY study abroad program. Deadlines: For Winter and Spring: October 1. For Summer and Fall: March 15.

After acceptance to a CUNY program, submit an ePermit through CUNYfirst. Register for STABD credits — placeholder credits for study abroad — not for a specific academic course code. Check the status of your ePermit to confirm it has been processed and approved.

--- ACADEMIC POLICIES ---

- Letter grades from abroad will appear on your Hunter transcript but will NOT count towards the Hunter GPA.
- Credit will be awarded based on the U.S. equivalent of the grade received abroad.
- Courses taken as Pass/Fail will not transfer.
- If your converted grade is below C (C- to F), it will appear on your Hunter transcript as F (this policy applies to programs prior to Summer 2025).

All courses taken abroad need academic department approvals for credit towards your Hunter degree. Submit a digital course approval form through the study abroad portal before going abroad.

--- WITHDRAWALS ---

Notification of withdrawal must be made via the application portal. Select "request to withdraw" and provide a reason. The Education Abroad team will confirm via email. The date the written withdrawal request is received is used for financial calculations. Students who withdraw after the start date are responsible for the entire program fee. There are no refunds for late arrivals or early departures.

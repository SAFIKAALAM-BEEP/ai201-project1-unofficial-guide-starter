GREEK LIFE
Hunter has a small Greek life presence. Several fraternities and sororities are active, though Hunter does not have a Greek Row or dedicated Greek housing. Greek organizations host philanthropic events, networking opportunities, and social events throughout the year.

HOW TO JOIN A CLUB
Students can find clubs at the annual Involvement Fair held at the start of each semester. Most clubs post meeting times on their Instagram accounts or through the Hunter student portal. To start a new club, students must submit a club charter through the Office of Student Activities with at least 10 founding members and a faculty advisor.
